## 14-day execution checklist

1. Day 1: Post in one relevant Facebook or suburb group after checking its self-promotion rules.
2. Day 2: Post in one renovation forum or Reddit community using the feedback-first version.
3. Days 3–5: Personally invite five homeowners who currently have quotes to test it.
4. Days 6–7: Contact two building inspectors and two buyer’s advocates for product feedback.
5. Day 8: Review completions, helpful/not-helpful votes and waitlist sign-ups.
6. Days 9–11: Rewrite the weakest explanation based on feedback; do not add features yet.
7. Days 12–14: Repeat the best-performing post in two new, relevant communities.

Success threshold after 14 days: 100 visitors, 20 completed checks, five feedback responses and three requests for real document analysis.
