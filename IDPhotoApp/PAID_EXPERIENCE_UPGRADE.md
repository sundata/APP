# Paid experience upgrade

This release moves the app from a one-off photo utility to a reusable, private ID-photo workspace.

- Local projects with automatic saving and recoverable edit settings
- Revision-safe rendering and undo/redo
- Precise crop calculations and true 300 DPI pixel output
- On-device photo checks and background edge repair
- Natural, restrained correction controls
- Australian passport preset and official guidance
- Australian English localisation and App Store metadata
- Privacy copy aligned with on-device processing and no advertising

Acceptance by passport, visa or licensing authorities is never guaranteed; official requirements remain authoritative.
