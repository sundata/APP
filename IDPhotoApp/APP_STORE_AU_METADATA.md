## Description
Create polished ID and passport photos directly on your iPhone. Choose an Australian passport crop or another common international size, follow composition checks, refine the crop and background, and export at 300 DPI.

- Australian passport guidance with an official-source link
- On-device face and composition checks
- Precise crop, rotation and background edge repair
- Natural adjustments designed to preserve identity
- JPEG and PNG export plus print layouts
- Projects saved locally on your device
- No ads; photos are not uploaded to our servers

ID Photo Studio is an editing aid. Requirements can change and acceptance is always determined by the relevant authority. Australian passport photos must not be digitally retouched; verify the latest rules before applying.
