# Privacy Policy — ID Photo Studio

Last updated: 30 August 2026

ID Photo Studio processes selected photos and camera frames on the user's device to create ID photos. Photos are not uploaded to our servers. Face detection used for composition guidance is performed on-device and is not used to identify a person.

The app requests camera access only to take a photo, photo-library read access only when the user chooses an existing photo, and add access only when the user saves a result. Projects and temporary editing data remain in the app's local storage and can be removed by deleting projects or the app.

The app does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information. Apple may process purchase and diagnostic information under Apple's own policies.

This app assists with sizing and composition but does not guarantee acceptance by an issuing authority. For Australian passports, check the current Australian Passport Office requirements.
